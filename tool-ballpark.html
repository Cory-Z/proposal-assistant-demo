How the range is built

Low: solid client screener draft, standard protocol complexity, no prep buffer, 15-minute breaks, facilitator 6 hrs/day, few critical tasks and use errors. High: poor or no screener draft, moderately complex protocol (×1.25), 10% prep buffer, 30-minute breaks, facilitator 8 hrs/day, many critical tasks and a standard number of use errors needing root cause analysis. Other deliverables run from the small to the large scope tier. Project management is 15% of consulting fees (minimum $4,000). Recruitment and incentive rates by participant type: patients/lay users $120 / $60, nurses/HCPs $200 / $80, physicians/surgeons $400 / $320. Ranges are rounded out to the nearest $800.
